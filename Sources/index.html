Application mode

Disconnect before switching. Hardware modes reopen disconnected; click Connect when ready.

Scanning…Find your KPA1500 using its USB cable or your local network. After the search, choose it from the list and click Connect.

Disconnect first. Searches available USB ports at the selected baud and the local IPv4 network (up to 254 addresses) at the selected TCP port. Identification only; nothing connects automatically. Powered-off or busy amplifiers may not appear. Manual settings remain available.

The amplifier’s MAC address is read and saved automatically when connected. For first-time startup while off, enter NET MAC manually. Ethernet Power On uses Wake-on-LAN; enable NET WAKE on LAN on the amplifier. USB Power On uses the selected Host PC port. Rear power switch and AC must remain on. Startup mode follows the amplifier’s POWER ON MODE setting.

Share live amplifier readings with the DX Caliber Logger’s Station Accessories panel on this computer. The Logger can display these readings but cannot control the amplifier through this connection.

Uses the saved connection type and settings. One attempt per launch; no automatic retries.

USB baud must match SERIAL SPEED HOST. For TCP/IP, enter the amplifier’s NET IP ADDRESS and command port (normally 1500). Only one TCP client can connect at a time. Connect saves the selected hardware connection. The app starts disconnected unless auto-connect is enabled and never changes your baud setting. Each command is checked and sent once; reconnecting does not replay controls.

Start ATU tune?
The amplifier may request tuning RF through its existing hardware connections. Check the antenna and radio’s tune setup before starting.


Power off amplifier?
The main amplifier supplies will switch off. RF must be off and tuning stopped. USB can turn it back on; Ethernet startup requires saved Wake-on-LAN settings. The rear power switch must remain on.
